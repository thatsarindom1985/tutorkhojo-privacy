TutorKhojo Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data TutorKhojo collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

About TutorKhojo

TutorKhojo (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a mobile app, operated by Adishrii EduTech, that helps parents and students find tutors in their area and lets tutors create a profile so families can find them. This policy applies to the TutorKhojo Android app (package name com.adishriiedutech.tutorkhojo).

1. Information we collect

  • Google account data: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Phone number: you enter a 10-digit mobile number in the app so tutors and families can connect with you.
  • Profile information: your role (tutor, parent or student), full name, profile photo, subjects taught or required, locality, and education details that you choose to provide.
  • Location: if you allow location access, the app reads your device’s approximate location to fill in your city or locality on the home screen. You can refuse or turn off location permission in your device settings and enter your locality manually.
  • Photos: pictures you choose to upload for your profile. We reduce their size before uploading.
  • Usage and device data: app interactions, search queries, device type and operating system version, used to improve the app and fix problems.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account: Google sign-in verifies who you are and signs you in.
  • Connect tutors and families: matching tutors with parents and students by location, subjects and requirements.
  • Communication: letting tutors and parents or students contact each other.
  • Improve the app: understanding usage to add features and fix bugs.
  • Notifications: telling you about messages, requirement updates and tutor responses, where this feature is enabled and based on your preferences.

3. Google user data

TutorKhojo uses Google Sign-In and requests only basic account information: your name, email address and profile picture.

  • We use this data only to create your account, sign you in and show your name and photo in your profile.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to others except as described in this policy (storage with our service provider and legal requirements).
  • We do not read your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or other Google services.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Information sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With other users: your profile information (name, subjects, locality, ratings) is visible to other users because this is how the platform works. Phone number visibility is controlled by your privacy settings in the app.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase for data storage and authentication. Our providers process data only to run the service for us.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if the law, a court order or a government authority requires it.

5. Data security

  • Secure storage: your data is stored with Supabase, with Row Level Security policies that limit access.
  • Encrypted transmission: data sent between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access controls: users can access their own data and the profile information that other users choose to make public.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: view and edit your profile in Account Settings.
  • Phone visibility: choose whether your phone number is visible to other users in Privacy Controls.
  • Profile visibility: turn your profile’s visibility in search results on or off in Account Settings.
  • Notifications: manage which notifications you receive in Account Settings.
  • Location: allow or deny location permission in your device settings at any time.
  • Account and data deletion: email us at adishriinexus@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and ask for deletion. You can also revoke TutorKhojo’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children’s privacy

TutorKhojo connects tutors with families. Students may use the app, but accounts for children under 13 must be created and managed by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has given us information without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

We keep your personal data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it or where we need it for legitimate purposes such as resolving disputes.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use TutorKhojo after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal data, email adishriinexus@gmail.com.
