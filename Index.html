TutorKhojo Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how TutorKhojo ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

TutorKhojo collects the following types of information when you use our application:

  • Phone Number: Used for OTP-based authentication and account identification.
  • Profile Information: Full name, profile photo, subjects taught/required, locality, and educational details you voluntarily provide.
  • Role Information: Whether you are a Tutor, Parent, or Student, to personalise your experience.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, search queries, and feature usage to improve our service.
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system version for compatibility and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate Users: Verify your identity via OTP and manage your account securely.
  • Connect Tutors & Families: Match tutors with parents and students based on location, subjects, and requirements.
  • Enable Messaging: Facilitate in-app communication between tutors and parents/students.
  • Improve the App: Analyse usage patterns to enhance features, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Send Notifications: Notify you about messages, requirement updates, and tutor responses (based on your preferences).

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information (name, subjects, locality, ratings) is visible to other users as part of the platform's core functionality. Phone number visibility is controlled by your privacy settings.
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase for secure data storage and authentication. These providers are bound by strict data protection agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

4. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard measures:

  • Encrypted Storage: All data is stored securely using Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • HTTPS Transmission: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access Controls: Users can only access their own data and publicly shared profile information.

5. Your Rights & Controls

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Update: View and update your profile information at any time through Account Settings.
  • Phone Visibility: Control whether your phone number is visible to other users via Privacy Controls in Account Settings.
  • Profile Visibility: Toggle your profile's visibility in search results through Account Settings.
  • Notification Preferences: Manage which notifications you receive through Account Settings.
  • Account Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at adishriinexus@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

TutorKhojo is designed to connect tutors with families. While students may use the app, accounts must be created and managed by adults (parents or guardians) on behalf of minors under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as resolving disputes).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice within the app or sending a notification. Your continued use of TutorKhojo after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: adishriinexus@gmail.com
  • App: TutorKhojo — Account Settings > Help
